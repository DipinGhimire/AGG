Our Approach

Connecting culture and community through food

AGG LLC is a Japan-based company founded by people with roots in Nepal. Through our restaurants, we create welcoming places where people from different backgrounds can meet naturally.

We currently operate two restaurants in Saitama and Tokyo. Alongside serving local customers delicious food and warm hospitality, we continue to explore new opportunities that bring people together through food.
