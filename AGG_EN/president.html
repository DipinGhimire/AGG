President

A message from our president

Narayan Khadka, Representative Member of AGG LLC, shares his journey and vision.

President's Message

Narayan Khadka

I am Narayan Khadka, Representative Member of AGG LLC. Thank you for your continued support.

In Nepal, I built a foundation in hospitality through work at restaurants and a five-star hotel.

I later came to Japan to study. After four years at a Japanese language school and vocational college, I worked as a company employee for three years, gaining valuable practical experience.

About three years ago, I began gradually building my own business while continuing restaurant operations in Nepal. Those experiences eventually led to the establishment of AGG LLC.

Career

My journey

  • Born and raised in Nepal
  • Gained restaurant industry experience in Nepal
  • Worked at a five-star hotel
  • Studied in Japan for four years at a language school and vocational college
  • Worked as a company employee in Japan for three years after graduation
  • Began developing my own business around three years ago
  • Continues to operate a restaurant in Nepal
  • Founded AGG LLC and became its Representative Member

Having experienced both Nepalese and Japanese cultures, I value the perspectives that come from understanding each. Through our restaurant operations and other ventures, we will continue working to provide services that bring joy to our customers and communities. I sincerely appreciate your continued support.
